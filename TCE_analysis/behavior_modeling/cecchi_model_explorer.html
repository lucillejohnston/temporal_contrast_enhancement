Cecchi Model Explorer

The second-order pain model from Cecchi et al. 2012 and its first-order simplification, driven by real thermode stimuli. Move the parameters and watch both predictions against what subjects reported.

Eq. 1 · Full model
p̈  =  α·F(T,θ)  −  β·ṗ  +  γ·(Ṫ − λ)·p
F(T,θ) = max(0, T − θ)
drive — heat above threshold pushes pain up damping — resists any change in pain rate term — responds to how fast temperature is changing, and pulls pain back toward zero
Eq. 2 · Simplified (first-order)
ṗ  =  ᾱ·F(T,θ)  −  γ̄·p
ᾱ = α/β  ·  γ̄ = γλ/β
  1. Strong damping. Pain settles within 1/β s, far faster than the stimulus changes, so p̈ ≈ 0.
  2. λ ≫ |Ṫ|. The alarm rate is much larger than any real ramp, so Ṫ − λ ≈ −λ: the rate term loses its rate sensitivity and becomes a constant leak −γλ·p.
Set p̈ = 0, drop Ṫ, divide by β. The green dashed curve is built from the current slider values.
